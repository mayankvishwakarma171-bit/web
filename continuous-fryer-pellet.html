Continuous Namkeen & Pellet Fryer with Pellet Heat Exchanger

Industrial continuous frying system with multi-layer flight conveyor, sediment removal, external high-efficiency pellet heat exchanger, and centralized touchscreen PLC.

The Kravionex Continuous Namkeen & Pellet Fryer is the cornerstone of industrial snack factories. Raw extruded dough strands, boondi drops, or fryum pellets enter one end of the fryer and traverse through temperature-controlled oil via synchronized submersing flights. Dwell time is precisely regulated through variable frequency motor drives, ensuring 100% consistent moisture extraction and golden color.
