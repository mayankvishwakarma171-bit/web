Industrial Abrasive Potato and Root Vegetable Peeler

Food-grade vertical abrasive peeler with carborundum-lined chamber, continuous water flushing spray, quick discharge door, and stainless steel structural enclosure.

The Kravionex Industrial Abrasive Peeler employs durable silicon carbide carborundum bonded to the base rotor and chamber walls. As potatoes spin against the abrasive surfaces, continuous water sprays wash peels down into a collection filter, leaving clean peeled tubers ready for slicing.
