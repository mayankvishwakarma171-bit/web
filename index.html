About Kravionex Machinery LLP

Rooted in Maharashtra, Engineering for Food Processors Nationwide

"We believe that industrial food machinery must deliver predictable crispness, uncompromising hygienic safety, and lowest operating cost per kilogram."

Based in the manufacturing hub of Maharashtra, Kravionex Machinery LLP was established with a focused mission: to provide Indian and international snack producers with world-class, precision-engineered food-processing equipment that matches global sanitary standards while offering superior thermal economy.

Whether you are an established namkeen house upgrading from manual wood or diesel kadhais to automated continuous lines, or an ambitious food enterprise setting up a modern potato chips plant, our engineering team assists you at every stage — from CAD plant drawings and thermal calculations to machinery erection, FAT trials, and lifetime spare support.

Our Core Commitments

Engineering Focus
Precision Thermal Control

Low delta-T heat exchangers prevent thermal shock, hotspots, and oil scorching, preserving the natural color and taste of fried snacks.

Hygienic Sanitary Construction

Built exclusively with certified AISI 304 / 316 food-grade stainless steel with smooth, crevice-free TIG argon welds and Clean-In-Place (CIP) accessibility.

Oil Conservation Engineering

Low system oil volumes combined with high turnover rates significantly retard Free Fatty Acid (FFA) buildup, reducing oil wastage by up to 30%.

Responsive Commissioning & Spares

Factory-trained technicians ensure rapid on-site commissioning, operator training, and 24/7 technical hotline support from our Maharashtra facility.

Manufacturing Infrastructure

Built with Engineering Rigor at Every Stage

Our Maharashtra manufacturing facility combines CAD 3D modeling, precision laser cutting, food-grade sanitary fabrication, and rigorous thermal testing to deliver machinery that operates dependably under harsh industrial conditions.

SolidWorks CAD Modeling

Every machine and complete turnkey plant layout is modeled in 3D CAD to verify operator ergonomics, piping hydraulics, thermal expansion allowances, and factory floor clearances.

CNC Laser & Forming

Heavy-gauge stainless steel sheets are cut with CNC fiber laser cutters and precision press brakes, ensuring tight joint fitments, uniform gaps, and robust structural longevity.

Food-Grade TIG Argon Welding

All food-contact seams undergo purged TIG argon welding followed by sanitary grinding and pickling/passivation to eliminate bacterial harborage crevices.

FAT & Thermal Testing

Prior to plant dispatch, every system undergoes extensive Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT), including hydrostatic piping tests, pump flow verification, and electrical safety audits.

GMP & HACCP Aligned

Hygienic sanitary design principles preventing food contamination.

Traceable Material Certs

Certified mill test certificates for AISI 304 / 316 stainless steel.

Precision Thermal Efficiency

Designed for low oil turnover and optimal fuel economy across continuous production cycles.
