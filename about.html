Our Heritage & Identity

A Modern Industrial Force in Food Machinery Engineering

"Snack manufacturing is an exact science where thermal stability, rapid oil turnover, and sanitary stainless steel fabrication dictate the market success of every crisp."

The Indian snack and namkeen industry is one of the fastest-growing and most competitive sectors in global food processing. For decades, manufacturers faced an unfortunate dilemma: either invest in exorbitantly priced European machinery with complex imported spares, or rely on unstandardized local fabrications plagued by temperature fluctuations, frequent breakdowns, high oil degradation, and prohibitive fuel expenses.

Kravionex Machinery LLP was established in Maharashtra to eliminate this compromise. By integrating advanced 3D SolidWorks engineering, CNC laser fabrication, food-grade AISI 304/316 metallurgy, and cutting-edge biomass pellet heat exchanger technology, we deliver machinery that rivals international standards at accessible Indian manufacturing economics.

Today, our equipment spans from compact circular and rectangular batch fryers for regional snack innovators to fully automated continuous frying lines producing hundreds of kilograms of nylon sev, boondi, potato chips, and extruded snacks per hour.

Our Core Engineering Philosophy

The Four Pillars of Kravionex Equipment Design

Every batch kettle, continuous conveyor, peeler, slicer, and turnkey line leaving our Maharashtra shop floor is guided by four uncompromising design mandates:

1. Precision Thermal Stability

Cooking oil degrades when exposed to localized thermal hotspots. Our external multi-pass shell & tube and biomass heat exchangers ensure uniform heating with a minimal delta-T across the entire oil bath, preventing scorching and preserving snack aroma.

2. Sanitary Food-Grade Metallurgy

All product-contact surfaces are fabricated exclusively from certified AISI 304 and AISI 316 food-grade stainless steel. All welds are purged TIG argon welded and finished crevice-free to conform with strict GMP and HACCP hygiene guidelines.

3. Radical Fuel & Operating Economy

Fuel represents up to 40% of snack manufacturing operating expense. Kravionex is a pioneer in integrating automated biomass pellet heating systems that reduce fuel expenditure by 45% to 60% compared to conventional commercial LPG or diesel.

4. Low Oil Turnover & Longevity

By optimizing oil depth, velocity, and rapid in-line mesh filtration, our fryers maintain low Free Fatty Acids (FFA) and low peroxide levels, allowing commercial producers to extend oil lifespan and eliminate rancidity in packaged shelf stock.

Turnkey Project Lifecycle

From Concept & Sizing to Commercial Plant Commissioning

We don't merely ship machinery in crates. Kravionex partners with your team as a technical manufacturing ally through a structured 6-phase engineering process.

Phase 01

Consultation & Capacity Sizing

We evaluate your raw material recipes, target packaging volumes, oil turnover rates, floor dimensions, and available utilities (power, water, chimney height, fuel choice).

Phase 02

Custom 3D CAD Plant Layout

Our design engineers generate 2D & 3D plant layouts illustrating raw material infeed, frying zones, de-oiling, cooling, seasoning, and packaging machine infeed conveyors.

Phase 03

Sanitary Fabrication in Maharashtra

Components are laser-cut and welded under strict quality control. Heat exchangers are hydro-tested and insulated with high-density ceramic wool to eliminate radiation heat loss.

Phase 04

Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT)

Clients are invited to inspect trial dry-runs and water circulation tests at our Maharashtra manufacturing facility prior to machinery dispatch.

Phase 05

On-Site Erection & Commissioning

Kravionex commissioning engineers travel to your facility to oversee mechanical leveling, piping hookups, electrical wiring, and conduct live production trial batches.

Phase 06

Operator Training & Lifetime Spares

We train your floor operators on CIP sanitization, temperature recipe setting, and routine maintenance, backed by prompt spare part dispatch from our Maharashtra inventory.
